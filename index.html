How to read the seat numbers

  • HMI and NIMAS publish a live seats-remaining count per batch; those are the figures shown, captured 5 September 2026, and they move daily.
  • NIM, JIM and ABVIMAS publish sanctioned intake per course and mark a batch “full” or “closed” — the number in the seats column is capacity, not vacancy.
  • SGMI, HAWS and MSI Auli publish nothing; the tables record the course structure that is documented and say so where a figure does not exist.
  • Fees are Indian-national rates unless noted. HMI, NIM and JIM subsidise most seats and sell a small number at actual cost; ABVIMAS and NIMAS charge foreign nationals 3–4×, and NIMAS currently admits Indian nationals only.
